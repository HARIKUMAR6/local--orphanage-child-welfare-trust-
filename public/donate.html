    Frequently asked questions

    Clear answers before you give.

    Still have a question?
    How can I donate?

    This website does not take online payments yet. Send us an enquiry or call us, and we will share the available giving options and confirm the details with you directly.

    Can I give every month?

    Yes, you can ask about monthly giving. Tell us what suits you in your enquiry and we will explain the next steps.

    Can I donate items instead of money?

    Gifts in kind such as books, stationery and groceries may be possible. Please contact us first so we can tell you what is most useful.

    Is my donation tax-deductible?

    We have not published any tax-exemption details on this website. Please contact us and ask before you give.

    Where do I send my donation?

    Please do not send money to any account you have not confirmed with us directly. We share payment details only after you contact us.

    Can I visit or volunteer?

    Please get in touch first. We will arrange a suitable time and explain how we keep children safe during visits.
