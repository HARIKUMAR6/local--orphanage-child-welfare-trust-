Education and learning

    Admission help, uniforms and supplies.

  • Books and learning tools

    Reading materials within easy reach.

  • Reading and digital skills

02 Program

Nutrition and daily care

Healthy meals and daily routines help children grow strong.

  • Balanced daily meals

    Wholesome food, served with care.

  • Fresh fruit and vegetables

    Seasonal produce wherever possible.

  • Healthy eating habits

    Learning to enjoy good food.

  • Safe food preparation

    Clean kitchens and careful handling.

03 Program

Health and wellbeing

Caring for the body and the mind, every day.

  • Regular check-ups

    Routine health reviews for every child.

  • First aid and basic care

    Quick help for everyday illness and injury.

  • Referral to doctors

    Specialist care when it is needed.

  • Hygiene and emotional wellbeing

    Healthy habits and a listening ear.

  • Safe, supervised play

    Time to be a child, in safe surroundings.

  • Creative and life-skill activities

  • Safeguarding practices

  • Counselling and family support
